## Decisions

- 2026-05-28: UX targets Android mobile first.
- 2026-05-28: Use a practical minimalist Material 3 direction to support low-end Android devices and non-technical users.
- 2026-05-28: Use bottom navigation for Dashboard, Products, History, and Settings.
- 2026-05-28: Use product-row actions for Stock In, Stock Out, and Edit to reduce taps.
- 2026-05-28: Treat stock movement flows as high-priority operational flows; avoid ads, blocking upsells, or extra modal steps inside them.
- 2026-05-28: Prioritize readable typography, clear warnings, and 48dp minimum touch targets.

## Assumptions

- App visual language is English-first for MVP.
- Currency display uses Philippine peso context.
- Login screen remains future-ready and is not shown by default in MVP.
- App name remains generic in UX artifacts until final branding is selected.
